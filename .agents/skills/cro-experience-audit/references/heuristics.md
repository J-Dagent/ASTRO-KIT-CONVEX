# CRO heuristics

Treat these as candidate mechanisms, never universal requirements.

Evaluate whether the context supports:
- a benefit-led hero and direct next action;
- action accessibility without unnecessary intermediate steps;
- proof close to important claims and conversion points;
- objection handling and risk reduction supported by real evidence;
- appropriate navigation/escape routes for the traffic intent;
- justified form placement, field count, steps, and qualification friction;
- clear mobile action accessibility and fast loading;
- a coherent thank-you/follow-up path;
- genuinely differentiated intent-specific routes when they provide distinct value.

Assume visitors scan. Put critical persuasion in headings, bullets, proof captions, CTA copy, and objection answers.

Use session recordings, heatmaps, and qualitative behavior as diagnostic evidence. Use controlled tests or stronger downstream evidence for causal claims.

Never manufacture proof, guarantees, testimonials, logos, rankings, or performance claims to satisfy a heuristic.
