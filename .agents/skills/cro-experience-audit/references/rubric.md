# Audit rubric

Score evidence, not aesthetics.

Audit dimensions: traffic/intent fit, message match, offer clarity, conversion-action fit, above-fold comprehension, form access and justified friction, proof quality/proximity, objections/risk, mobile usability, performance/clarity, tracking readiness, and downstream quality.

For each material finding record:
- observation;
- evidence;
- severity: `BLOCKER | HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW`;
- mechanism;
- recommended change;
- test hypothesis when appropriate;
- measurement requirement;
- owner/handoff.

Prioritize root causes over presentation polish. Do not turn an evidence gap into a recommendation stated as fact.
