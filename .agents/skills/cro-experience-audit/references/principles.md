# CRO principles

## Evidence precedence

Prefer current first-party evidence and explicit owner decisions over generic heuristics. Keep observed fact, source-derived heuristic, and inference distinct. Missing business context becomes `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, or `BLOCKED`, not an invented conclusion.

## Business truth

When available, prioritize revenue/sales outcome, qualified opportunity or lead quality, booked/attended next step, lead conversion, then click/engagement proxies. Do not call a winner from a weaker proxy when stronger evidence is part of the decision rule.

## Intent and message match

Classify intent before tactics. Distinguish brand/high-intent, non-brand acquisition, mixed automated traffic, cold prospecting, retargeting, organic, and warm/direct contexts without making channel-specific rules universal.

Trace continuity through acquisition promise → page → form/CTA expectation → thank-you/follow-up → sales reality.

## Useful friction

A field, step, eligibility gate, or longer page may be justified when it materially supports qualification, trust, conversion, attribution, operations, compliance, or downstream quality. Do not remove friction automatically.

## Ownership

Strategy owns audience, offer, promise, approved copy/proof, CRO hypothesis, and business success criteria. Measurement owns what should be measured. Delivery owns implementation and technical reliability. Route missing decisions back to their owner.
